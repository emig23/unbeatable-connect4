About this project

A Connect 4 game with an AI that solves the game as it plays. On Unbeatable, when it moves first, it cannot lose.

Difficulty levels

Easy
Looks 2 moves ahead and plays loosely
Medium
Looks about 9 moves ahead
Unbeatable
Solves the game perfectly

How it works

  • Connect 4 is a solved game: the first player can always win by starting in the center.
  • The Unbeatable AI searches every position to the end of the game, sped up with bitboards, a transposition table and a precomputed opening book.
  • The win chance bar simulates hundreds of games early on, then shows the exact result once the position can be solved.
